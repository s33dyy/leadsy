# Lead Magnet SOP

This is the operating procedure for turning Leadsy into a lead magnet, not only a lead manager.

## Goal

Create new opportunities for the agency owner, research public business leads deeply, draft the right first touch, and keep humans focused on leads that can realistically convert.

## Daily Operating Loop

1. Save the agency lead brief: what you sell, who to find, where to search, lead goal, and bad-fit exclusions.
2. Run free public research across OpenRouter web search/fetch, free business directories, public social profiles, contact pages, review/reputation pages, content gaps, hiring/news signals, competitor context, public page extraction, and imported real lists.
3. Build a lead dossier for every lead: contact details only when publicly found, evidence links, fit score, confidence, reason, and next action.
4. Apply guardrails. Public-only leads stay in manual review; AI can draft, but automated sending is blocked until explicitly approved.
5. Draft first touch. AI drafts a short WhatsApp/DM/email message based on public evidence, agency offer, language, and likely intent.
6. Qualify replies. AI asks for budget, location, timeline, requirement, appointment preference, and language.
7. Route hot leads. High-intent replies go to the owner or closer with a summary and suggested action.
8. Nurture softer leads. AI schedules reminders, sends relevant options, and prevents dead-lead drift.
9. Report outcomes. Agency reports should show source, reply rate, qualified rate, booking rate, and follow-up SLA.

## Messaging Policy

AI should be aggressive with free public research, scoring, personalization, summarization, and follow-up planning. It must not invent missing contact details, use paid OSINT/data broker sources, bypass logins, scrape private accounts, or auto-send spam. When in doubt, block the send and recommend manual review or retargeting.

## Core KPIs

- Prospects found per client
- Outreach-ready prospects
- First-response time
- Reply rate
- Qualification rate
- Booking rate
- Cost per qualified lead
- Cost per booked appointment
- Human escalation accuracy
