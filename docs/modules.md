# Modules

## AI Lead Command Center

Operations view for Meta CPL, speed-to-lead, AI follow-up volume, qualified leads, bookings, client health, and WhatsApp priority conversations.

## Lead Magnet Engine

Autonomous prospect discovery for agencies. It monitors approved sources, creates discovered-lead candidates, scores fit and urgency, drafts WhatsApp/DM outreach, blocks unsafe outreach, and moves approved prospects into qualification and follow-up.

## Meta Lead Ingestion

Instagram and Facebook lead webhooks are normalized, deduped, mapped to agency client workspaces, attributed to campaign/ad set/creative, and immediately pushed into qualification.

## WhatsApp Inbox

First-class WhatsApp workflow with AI-assisted replies, multilingual readiness, voice-note architecture, smart follow-ups, booking handoffs, unread status, and conversation summaries.

## AI Lead Qualification

Scores budget, location fit, urgency, buying intent, sentiment, language, and spam risk. The AI recommends whether to nurture, escalate, call, or book.

## Agency Multi-Client

Supports isolated client workspaces, campaign-level analytics, white-label readiness, client reporting, and AI-generated performance summaries.

## AI Lead Intelligence Engine

Includes account enrichment, contact enrichment, provider waterfall design, social scraping abstraction, ICP scoring, intent scoring, buying signal detection, job-change detection, duplicate resolution, and contact verification.

## CRM

Includes accounts, contacts, deals, tasks-ready data model, notes-ready timeline, activities, relationship graph, smart filters, drag/drop pipeline state, and command/copolot entry points.

## Outreach

Supports cold email sequences, LinkedIn workflow steps, call tasks, WhatsApp branches, AI personalization, copy generation interfaces, sequence branching, inbox rotation simulation, deliverability scoring, and campaign analytics.

## Copilot

System-wide assistant for account summaries, outreach, forecasts, workflow generation, CRM questions, smart filters, and queued actions. API route: `POST /api/copilot`.

## Capture

Models embeddable forms, conversational widgets, AI chat widgets, visitor deanonymization, routing rules, booking flows, and qualification flows.

## Automation Builder

Typed workflow canvas with trigger, enrichment, AI, routing, CRM, and messaging nodes. API route: `POST /api/workflows/run`.

## Analytics

Custom dashboard surface for cohorts, funnel conversion, attribution, SDR performance, forecasting, and anomaly detection.
